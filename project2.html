METHOD & SOURCES

Make the reasoning
as visible as the result.

A clear distinction between what the prototype demonstrates and what the data can support.

01

Consistent denominators

Ratios are calculated as scenario population divided by scenario facility count, rounded to the nearest resident.

02

No false precision

Facilities are not individual clinicians. Zero facilities produces “Not defined,” rather than an arbitrary large number.

03

County health context

CDC requests specify location 53033 and crude prevalence. Confidence intervals and the returned observation year remain visible.

Data provenance & limitations +

Population and facility counts are illustrative records from the original portfolio and may use different geographic boundaries. This comparison does not measure workforce supply, appointment capacity, Medicaid acceptance, or actual shortages. It does not assign HRSA designations or infer an individual’s mental health from where they live.

  • Original portfolio dataset ↗

    Neighborhood scenarios are hard-coded in the original project. No row-level citations or extraction records accompany them.

  • CDC PLACES ↗

    County rows are filtered to King County (53033) and crude prevalence. Each indicator shows the year returned by the API.

  • HRSA · Shortage designation ↗

    Official designations require a separate assessment; the prototype does not apply a home-made threshold.
