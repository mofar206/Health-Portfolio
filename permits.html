What the analysis reveals

The overall median application-to-issue interval is zero calendar days. But the BLD - BUILDING category has a median of 54 days among 544 records with valid dates. A single headline metric would obscure that difference.

The business recommendation

Segment the review discussion by permit type and current status before looking at age. Use an individual record’s milestones to ask a better question, rather than treating every older application as a delayed case.

Why: 1,832 of the 2,704 valid intervals are same-day records, which strongly shapes the overall median.

The product decision

Make the age marker adjustable and label it as hypothetical. An operations user can explore a candidate list without the interface implying an official deadline.

Tradeoff: age since application is available, while time in the current status is not. The interface keeps that distinction visible.

Requirements that matter

  • Every displayed record can be inspected by permit number.
  • Missing issue dates do not become zero-day intervals.
  • Filters and CSV export use the same record set.
  • No applicant names or addresses are needed for this analysis.

Before operational use

Review field definitions with permit staff, obtain status event history, and identify which cases are actionable. Then evaluate whether users can find the intended records and distinguish calendar age from staff processing time. No operational savings are claimed.
