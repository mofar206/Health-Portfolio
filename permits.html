What is in the active snapshot?

A useful operations view needs explicit status definitions and inspectable records. Filters support narrowing a review list by type, year, and current status without assigning a cause to older cases.

Define before measuring.

The pipeline retrieves every object ID, reconciles parcel rows by permit, and checks date order. SQL summarizes statuses and valid application-to-issue intervals. Missing issue dates are excluded from the median rather than treated as zero.

A review cue, with boundaries.

A configurable age marker makes prioritization assumptions visible. Before operational use, a next step would validate definitions with permit staff and obtain event history to distinguish applicant response time from city review time.

Scope & evidence

This is the city’s “Permit Case Parcels to Active” layer, not all historical applications. “Issued” does not mean completed. Calendar days from application to issue are not city processing time; days since application are not days in the current status. Historical throughput and staffing performance cannot be inferred from this snapshot.
