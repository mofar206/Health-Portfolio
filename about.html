WHY THIS WORK

Local questions.
Transferable skills.

This portfolio is a personal project about issues I care about in my own community: how healthcare, transit, and public-service systems can be difficult to navigate for underserved communities.

The approach connects to broader public-service and product work—gathering information, identifying gaps, building useful interfaces, and turning observations into questions that can be tested.

Each case study pairs an interactive prototype with its method and limitations, making both the design decisions and the boundaries of the evidence visible.
