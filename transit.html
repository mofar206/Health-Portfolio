What the analysis reveals

worth investigating.

At the selected Auburn anchor, scheduled departures fall from 387 on Tuesday to 199 on Sunday. That is a 48.6% decrease at the same stop group. Skyway’s selected anchor stays close to its weekday count: 132 on Tuesday and 134 on Sunday.

The product decision

Keep location and time window fixed when switching days. This makes a weekend comparison easier to interpret than a leaderboard of raw departure counts across differently sized stop groups.

Tradeoff: the tool answers a focused schedule question. It does not promise that a person can reach a particular destination.

The recommendation

Start the next research round with weekend journeys from the Auburn anchor. Ask residents about destinations, transfer constraints, and travel times before proposing a service or product change.

Decision boundary: fewer scheduled departures alone do not establish unmet demand or justify reallocating service.

What I would prioritize next

  1. First: validate the selected anchors and comparison task with potential users.
  2. Then: add destination-based journey examples using documented routing data.
  3. Later: incorporate reliability and accessibility information if suitable sources become available.

How I would evaluate it

Ask participants to compare weekday and Sunday service at the same anchor, identify a route, and explain one limitation. Measure task completion, time, and interpretation errors. These are planned measures, not claimed test results.
