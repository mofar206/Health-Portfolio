THE QUESTION

What would help someone take the next step?

I wanted to make health access information easier to explore while keeping the limits of the data visible.

THE APPROACH

Connect the interface to the evidence.

The tools use clear comparisons, calculated ratios, and separate public health context. Each exploration explains how its values should be read.

WHAT COMES NEXT

Build a stronger local picture.

The next step is replacing illustrative neighborhood records with documented local data, then testing which information people find useful when looking for services.

A note on the data

Neighborhood records are illustrative scenarios from the original project. They are not verified estimates or a directory of currently available care. Public CDC county indicators are shown separately with their observation years and source information.
